Conquest of AldemereI. The game

Chapter I

A road, a die, and a crowd to leave behind

You grew up on a farm at the edge of the Greywood. Today you stand in the square at Crownhall with ten thousand others while Ser Halvard Ashgrove is knighted. Everyone cheers. You are one voice among them, and you are nobody.

Conquest of Aldemere is a text adventure with painted scenes, turn-based fights and a story in three acts. Each risky choice and each blow is settled by a die. Your stats, your gear and your standing in the world tilt the odds, and no reroll saves you from a bad one.

I. The gameConquest of Aldemere

The dice decide

Might, Agility, Wits, Spirit and Charm shape every roll. A clever plan helps. A good die helps more.

A world you can see

Painted pixel scenes for every place, a living map that opens as the story does, and a bestiary of foes that earn their pictures.

Choices that stay

Who you help, who you spare and what you carry out of a room follow you into the next act. Several endings wait at the far end.

Conquest of AldemereII. The realm

Chapter II

The realm of Aldemere

Nine painted tiles make the whole land. In the game, each part stays under fog until the story reaches it.

II. The realmConquest of Aldemere
The Greywood
Home ground. Farms, a river mill, old stones and a road to Crownhall.
The Fenmarch
Drowned causeways, stilt houses and a tower in the reeds.
The Ashfang Peaks
Smelter camps, black ridges and a crater that smokes.
Saltreach
Salt pans and a harbour where ships come in from far away.
The Pale North
Barren, cold and mist-bound. Sealed until the final act.
The Southern Sea
Open water below the marsh. Rocks, seabirds and one wreck that someone surely remembers.
The Silver Shore
A coast of pale trees and tall halls, with watchtowers facing the mountains. Its story is not written yet.
Thrumhold
A city of dwarves under the mountains, on a map of its own.
