The last two tiles

23 October 2026

The map of Aldemere has no blank corners left. Two tiles were missing at the bottom: one under the Fenmarch, one under the Ashfang Peaks. Both are painted and in the game now, which makes nine in all, three by three.

The southern sea

Under the marsh the land ends and the sea begins. It is open water: dark swell, rocks, gulls and one wreck far out. There are no ports and no quests out there. Saltreach looks over it, and the coast between the two runs on without a seam.

The silver shore

Under the mountains, east of Saltreach, the coast turns into a country of silver-barked trees and tall pale halls, with rope bridges between the branches and a white city on the bay. The border facing the mountains is armed: watchtowers, forts and a camp on the cliffs. Inland it looks calm. Whose land it is, and what it wants, is not written yet, so I will leave the name off the page.

Joining them

These two took a second try. The first attempt stretched the paintings to fit and bent the halls out of shape, so I threw it away. The second works from a larger guide: the tile as a blank rectangle with the real neighbouring map drawn along its edges, so the result is cut out at full size, with nothing stretched.
