Marked

26 November 2026

Two days have changed the shape of the game more than the month before them. I will describe what is new and keep to myself where it is hidden.

Things that are only there for you

Some encounters now carry an option that appears for one hero and not another: because of what you carry, what you have done, who walks with you, what you have become. Nothing points to it. A few of them appear only in some runs, by luck alone. Several ask a price. If you have once knelt to nobody and then watched the dead kneel to you, you will know what I mean.

Holiness and Unholiness

There was already a mark the dark could leave on you. It now has an opposite, and the two are kept apart: the sky and the dark judge you separately, and a hero can be torn between them. Both are earned slowly, through what you choose. Both close some doors as they open others. Neither makes you hit harder.

The black market

A hero who has taken enough of the dark may be visited by the Gristle Peddler, a thing that walks as much on its hands as its feet and keeps its wares inside its own ribs. It sells eleven things no honest stall would: a hanged man’s hand that opens locks, a face to borrow, a knife that keeps a ledger, a small dried thing on a black cord that stirs when something is coming for you. It asks for gold, and then for something else as well: your sleep, your soul’s good name, or the ash of what you have killed in the night.

The night

Go far enough and the nights stop being quiet. You are warned first. Then something that was not built by anyone opens in the air, and what comes through is not human and has come for you.

Elsewhere

The buttons that were still plain are painted, two more characters have portraits, and the numbers were looked at hard: the classes sit much closer to one another, gold is scarcer, and fewer fights are over before they begin.

What is still ahead

Many of the new creatures wear borrowed paintings. The market wants a song of its own. Act III is outlined and not written. There is still no public demo.
